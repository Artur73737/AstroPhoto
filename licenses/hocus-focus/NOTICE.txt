Covered source: HocusFocusMath.hpp and HocusFocusMath.cpp, included alongside
this notice in binary packages, plus tests/unit/HocusFocusMathTests.cpp in the
AstroPhoto source repository at https://github.com/Artur73737/AstroPhoto.
These files adapt upstream weight regularization, the symmetric hyperbolic
model, Huber weighting, covariance/leave-one-out diagnostics and robust local
background-plane fitting. AstroPhoto supplies its own bounded native solver,
Canon control, photometric aperture/HFD calculation and ImGui interface.
This is an adaptation of selected algorithms, not the N.I.N.A. plugin or an
upstream-endorsed product. No N.I.N.A., ALGLIB or .NET binaries are bundled.
